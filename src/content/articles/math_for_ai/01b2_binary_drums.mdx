<Then>

Each container held double the one next to it, like the folds: 1, 2, 4, 8. A full one became a lit bulb, an empty slot a dark one. So 13 kg is 8 + 4 + 1, written **1101**.

Writing numbers with only 0 and 1 is called **binary**. Each bulb, one yes-or-no, is a **bit**.

## The end! Five bulbs, any table

Your phone keeps every dot of a photo the same way: 8 bulbs per dot, from dark to bright.

<EightBulbs />

* Mama's shelf is how you already write numbers: one digit per slot, and ten of anything moves up.
* Change "ten" to "two", and the shelf becomes a row of bulbs: that's **binary**.
* The sizes double, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, like a folded newspaper.
* 8 bits give 0 to 255, which is why a pixel's brightness stops at 255.
