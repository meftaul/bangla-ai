Every journey is a train ride

A chapter becomes a rail line. Each journey is the ride to the next station. When you pull into the platform, the station hands you a gift made from what you just learned, and the ticket for the next ride. At the junction at the end of the line, you open your trunk and build something with the gifts.

DETAIL

The ticket, up close

Modelled on the old cardboard counter ticket: buff card, guilloche print, a red rule, a rubber stamp and a tear-off stub. Every field is real data from your ride.

  1. Route is the next journey: from the station you're at to the next one, in Bangla and English with a three-letter station code.
  2. Class reflects the ride you just did. All exercises right first time gets স্নিগ্ধা (blue card). Otherwise শোভন চেয়ার (buff card). Nobody is held back.
  3. Fare is paid in what you learned, e.g. "one cosine similarity". It doubles as a one-line recap.
  4. Coach and seat are stable per learner, so your seat number is the same on every ticket, like your seat on a regular route.
  5. The stub gets punched on each exercise during the ride. The album keeps punched tickets.
SCREEN 4

The trunk and the junction workshop

Gifts go into a tin trunk that travels with you. At the junction at the end of the line, a blueprint asks for some of them. Tap a gift to fit it. When the blueprint is full, you build the line's tool and use it once, for real.

Build: a tiny search box

Type a question, and the box finds the library card that points the same way. It's the same move ChatGPT's attention makes, in miniature.

  • Each blueprint slot says which station the gift comes from, so a missing gift points you back to a journey.
  • The build is a real mini-exercise, using the gifts as its steps. It's the chapter's capstone.
  • Gifts carry across lines. Line 6 (matrices) could ask for the Line 4 shadow lamp again.

REVIEW

Questions for you

Ticket class by score?

Should ticket class reward first-try answers (স্নিগ্ধা vs শোভন চেয়ার), or should every ticket be the same so nobody feels graded?

Station names: story or topic?

This mockup names stations after each journey's story (হাট, সিনেমা হল). The alternative is topic names (Dot Product, Cosine).

What gets built?

One tool per line (search box for Line 4), or one big machine across the whole course that each line adds a part to?

How much motion on the ride?

Is the moving train in the progress bar enough, or do you want the train window with story scenery too?

Train names

Invented names like বিন্দু এক্সপ্রেস, or names that echo real intercity trains (সুবর্ণ, একতা, সুন্দরবন)?

Scope

Math for AI only to start, or every course in the library as a rail line?
